Privacy

Tilesday is built to keep your play on your device.

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your play stays on your device. Your saved game, lessons, stats, daily puzzles and settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • No ads, no tracking. Tilesday shares nothing with advertisers and never tracks you across other apps or websites. There are no ads.
  • Purchases. If you buy Tilesday Premium, Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Whether Premium is on is read on your device, from Apple.
  • Sales records. We count sales and subscriptions with RevenueCat. When the app opens, it checks in with RevenueCat using a random ID the app makes up, with device basics: the model, the iOS and app versions, your language and App Store country, and the ID Apple gives this app on your device. If you buy Premium, the App Store's record of the purchase goes there too: the plan, the price, the dates, renewals and refunds. This never includes your name, email, Apple ID or anything about your games, and it is never used for ads or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Ratings. The app may ask Apple to show its rating prompt; Apple handles your answer.

If a future version changes any of this, this page and the App Store privacy label will say exactly what is collected and why, before that version ships.
