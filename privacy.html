Privacy

Tilesday is built to keep your play on your device.

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your saved game, lessons, stats, daily puzzles and settings are stored on your iPhone or iPad only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Nothing is collected. Tilesday collects no personal data and shares nothing with advertisers. There are no ads.
  • Purchases. If you buy Tilesday Premium, Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The app only reads, on your device, whether Premium is on.
  • Ratings. The app may ask Apple to show its rating prompt; Apple handles your answer.

If a future version changes any of this, this page and the App Store privacy label will say exactly what is collected and why, before that version ships.
